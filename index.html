Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the grammar checker?

Our grammar checker uses advanced AI technology with an accuracy rate of over 95% for common grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors. It's continually trained on millions of text samples to improve its detection capabilities.

What types of documents can I check?

You can check essays, reports, emails, blog posts, academic papers, business documents, and more. Our tool supports various writing styles and formats up to 1000 characters at a time.

Is my text stored or used for training?

No, we prioritize your privacy. Text processed through our grammar checker is not stored permanently and is only used for real-time analysis. We don't use your content for training our AI models.

What languages are supported?

Currently, we support English (US, UK, AU, and CA variants) with plans to add Spanish, French, German, and other major languages in upcoming updates.

How does the AI-powered grammar check work?

Our system uses DeepSeek AI technology combined with natural language processing to analyze sentence structure, grammar rules, punctuation, spelling, and style recommendations. It compares your text against grammatical patterns and suggests corrections.
